How to use this QR code

  1. Open the camera on your phone.
  2. Point your camera at the QR code.
  3. Tap the link that appears on your screen.
  4. Enter your phone number.
  5. Tap the Call Owner button.
  6. You will receive a call from an unknown number.
  7. Answer the call. You will hear a message that your call is being connected to the vehicle owner.
  8. Stay on the call. You will be connected to the vehicle owner shortly.
